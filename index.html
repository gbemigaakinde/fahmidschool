Why Families Choose Us

What Makes Fahmid Different

Parents who choose Fahmid usually have a reason. Most of the time, it is more than one.

Small Class Sizes

Our classes are not large. Every teacher knows every child by name, and when a pupil is struggling, it does not take long for someone to notice and step in.

Teachers Who Care

Our staff are qualified, yes — but more than that, they are genuinely invested in these children. They do not just deliver lessons; they build relationships that help children grow in confidence.

Results That Show

Our pupils sit their exams and perform well. They go on to good secondary schools, and parents come back to tell us so. We are proud of that track record and we work hard to protect it.

Character Development

Good grades matter, but a child who is also respectful, honest, and confident will go further in life. We take character seriously at Fahmid — it runs through everything we do, not just assemblies.

Safe & Supervised

From the gate to the classroom to the playground, your child is watched over. We control who comes in and out, and our staff are always present. Parents tell us this peace of mind matters a great deal to them.

A Proper School Day

Children do better when they know what to expect. Our school day is well-structured and runs on time. Learning, activity, and rest each have their place — and the children respond well to that.
